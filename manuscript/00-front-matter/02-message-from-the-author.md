# A Message from the Author

Coding feels hard when you only look at it from one angle. Look at it a different way, as cooking, and it becomes much easier to learn.

Yes, it's confusing at first. Walk into a kitchen for the first time and you'll find so many ingredients that you don't know where to start, or even what you're going to cook. Code is the same. But every great cook started by learning one ingredient, one technique, one recipe at a time, and that's exactly how this book works.

I'm Yora Ji-hun, the author of this book, also known as "Jirrum," the same Chef Jirrum you'll meet in the kitchen. I dedicate this book to the Edica group and to every student who wants to pursue a career in coding. I wanted to make learning PHP fun by teaching it the way you'd learn to cook your favorite Filipino dish.

I love to eat, so I learned to cook when I was in Grade 3. I started coding in Grade 10. Over time I realized that skills can be combined, and that something you already understand can help you learn something new. That's the idea behind every chapter here.

**A quick note before we start:** technology changes every day, and there's always more than one way to solve a problem. Some of what's in this book may change as PHP keeps growing. But the habits and ways of thinking you'll build here will stay with you. Take these lessons as your starting point, and enjoy learning to code the fun way.

Happy cooking, and happy coding.

**Yora Ji-hun**

# What to Cook Next

A cook doesn't stop learning after the first menu. Here's where to go from here, one step at a time.

- **Build something real.** Pick a small project and finish it: an order form that saves to a file, a guestbook, or a simple menu page that reads from a JSON file. Finishing one small dish teaches more than starting ten.
- **Learn to use a database.** Text files work for practice, but real applications keep their data in a database. Look up PDO, PHP's built-in way to talk to databases like MySQL, and use prepared statements from day one.
- **Learn object-oriented PHP (OOP).** Classes and objects help you organize bigger projects, the way a recipe system helps a kitchen grow into a restaurant.
- **Try a framework.** Once OOP feels comfortable, a framework like Laravel gives you a well-organized kitchen with most of the tools already on the shelf.
- **Keep the good habits.** Validate every input. Escape every output. Name things clearly. These never go out of style, no matter what you build next.

And keep the PHP manual at php.net close by. It's the pantry label you can always check.

# Epilogue: Closing Time

The last order goes out. The stove clicks off, and the kitchen goes quiet for the first time all day.

Chef Jirrum wipes down the counter, the same one he was wiping on your first day. "Remember when you thought you were in the wrong place?"

You do. You also remember not knowing what `<?php` meant.

"Now you can take an order, check it, cook it, keep track of it, and handle it when something burns," he says. "That's not a student anymore. That's a cook."

He nods at the apron hanging by the door. It still looks a little too big. "Keep it. You'll grow into it."
